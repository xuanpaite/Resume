About

I understand the business problem, and I can build the AI that solves it.

For nearly ten years I've worked on the customer side of B2B companies: onboarding clients, building success roadmaps with clear metrics, running QBRs, renewals and upsells. Today I lead operations and customer success at Revnew.

Over the past year I've also been designing, building and running my own AI products. I use Claude for the AI features and Claude Code to build them, and I run everything myself in production, from security and backups to email deliverability.

That combination lets me speak to an executive about business value and to an engineering team about architecture in the same meeting.

How I build with AI

  • Start from the business problem and a success metric
  • Test whether the AI actually helps before relying on it
  • Keep a human in the loop and an audit trail on every decision
  • Choose models by cost and data sensitivity
  • Always have a fallback when the AI fails
